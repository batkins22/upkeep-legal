Purpose

Upkeep is a general wellness and habit-tracking app. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, monitor your health, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Do not use it for emergencies or to make medical decisions.

Hydration

Fluid needs vary. Follow any guidance or fluid restrictions from your healthcare professional. Do not drink extra water simply to complete a meter; excessive water intake can be harmful. Total-water reference values include food and all beverages and are not instructions to drink that amount as plain water.

Exercise

Choose activities, intensity and goals appropriate for your abilities and circumstances. The editable 150-minute weekly default is a tracking starting point, not an individualized prescription. Upkeep does not assess exercise intensity, fitness or whether you have met physical-activity guidelines. Do not push through concerning symptoms to meet a goal; stop and seek appropriate medical advice.

Outdoor time

Outdoor minutes are not a recommended dose of sunlight or UV exposure. Direct sun is not required. Use appropriate sun protection and consider heat, cold, weather and air quality. Upkeep does not monitor those conditions or determine how long it is safe for you to remain outside. Its suggested 20-minute goal is not a medical minimum or a guarantee of safe exposure.

Goals & emergencies

A completed meter or smiley means only that recorded activity reached a selected tracking amount. It does not establish adequate hydration, safe activity or a health benefit. Adapt, pause or skip goals when appropriate. If you think you have a medical emergency, contact local emergency services; Upkeep is not monitored for emergencies.
