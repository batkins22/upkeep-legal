About Upkeep

Upkeep is developed by Blair Atkins. It helps you record water intake, exercise minutes and outdoor time. You do not need an Upkeep account. The app does not include its own cloud synchronization service.

For privacy or support questions, contact support.upkeep@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Upkeep stores the information needed to provide its tracking features:

  • Water amounts and the dates and times of entries.
  • Exercise durations and the dates and times of entries, including entries you backdate.
  • Outdoor timer start/end times, manual outdoor minutes and the dates you select.
  • An active outdoor timer’s start time so it can recover after the app closes.
  • Your goals, quick-add presets, favorite presets, selected week-start day, tracker visibility and appearance preference.
  • Local technical records needed to initialize shared storage safely and prevent a stale outdoor widget action from starting a session again.

These records are kept in the app’s on-device storage. On iPhone, the app and its widgets use a shared App Group container to access the same tracking records. Older app versions and installations upgraded to shared storage may also retain local pre-upgrade recovery copies. The browser version stores records in that browser’s local storage, separately from iPhone data.

Upkeep uses the device’s local date, time and time-zone information to calculate daily and weekly totals. It does not use GPS or automatically detect when you are outdoors. The Male and Female total-water reference choices are settings, not a required personal profile.

How information is used and shared

The app uses your records locally to show totals, progress and history, run the outdoor timer, and perform the actions you select in the app or its widgets. The current app does not send tracking records to a developer-operated server, advertising service or analytics provider. It contains no advertising or analytics integration and does not sell your tracking records through an app feature.

Widgets and Live Activities can display information on your Lock Screen and other system surfaces. Other people may be able to see information displayed there. You can manage widgets and Live Activity visibility using your device’s controls. Apple controls any system-level display, distribution or synchronization behavior.

Upkeep does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, HealthKit, motion/fitness or notification access. Live Activity availability and Lock Screen authentication are controlled by iOS; they do not require location tracking.

External links and support

Settings includes links to the National Academies and Mayo Clinic hydration references. Opening a link uses a browser or browser view. Upkeep does not append your tracking records to those URLs. The website and browser may process ordinary web information, such as your IP address, cookies or information you choose to provide, under their own privacy policies.

If you email support.upkeep@gmail.com, the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send are processed to respond to your request. This voluntary support correspondence is separate from the tracking app. We retain it as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations.

Do not assume that app-local records are automatically included in a support request; Upkeep has no automatic log-upload feature in the current release.

Retention and deletion

Your active tracking records stay on your device until you remove them or the app’s storage is removed. You can delete individual entries in History. Disabling a tracker only hides it; it does not delete its records. Changing a goal or preset does not rewrite the amounts of previous entries.

Deleting an entry removes it from the active tracking history. It does not necessarily erase pre-upgrade recovery copies or copies in an existing device backup. Pre-upgrade local files are retained during the one-time shared-storage copy to protect existing records. The app does not offer a secure-erasure or delete-all-backups control.

Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, normally removes its local app data; shared-container cleanup is managed by iOS and depends on any remaining authorized apps/extensions. Other installations, browser data and device backups are separate. For browser use, clearing the tracker site’s local data removes that browser’s stored records. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase tracking records that remain only on your device.

Device backups and Apple services

App files may be included in iCloud or computer backups, depending on your device and backup settings. Restoring a backup may restore older records. Removing data in Upkeep does not remove an already-created backup; use Apple’s backup controls to manage those copies. Offloading an app can preserve its documents and data.

Apple may process information through the App Store, TestFlight, crash reporting, device backups or other system services under Apple’s terms and your device settings. For example, TestFlight can make beta feedback and crash information available to the developer. This is separate from Upkeep transmitting its tracking database to a server.

This Privacy and Support website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Its pages include no analytics, advertising scripts, third-party fonts, forms, accounts or application tracking storage. Visiting this website does not read the tracking records stored by the Upkeep app or its browser tracker.

GitHub states that GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not a visitor is signed into GitHub. That hosting activity is separate from Upkeep’s local tracking records. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Security

Upkeep relies on the operating system’s app sandbox and file-protection mechanisms. On iPhone, shared tracking files are configured to remain available after the first device unlock so authorized widget actions can work while the screen is locked. Device access, backup choices and operating-system security affect protection of local records. No app can guarantee absolute security or recovery of lost device data.

Policy changes and questions

We may update this policy if Upkeep’s features or data practices change. The published policy will show its effective date.
