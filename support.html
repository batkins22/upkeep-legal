Reporting a bug

A few details help us understand what happened:

  1. Describe what you were doing and the steps that reproduce the issue.
  2. Tell us what you expected and what actually happened.
  3. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and Upkeep version/build number, if available. For a beta build, check TestFlight.
  4. Optionally attach a screenshot with personal information removed.

Please do not send passwords, signing credentials, device serial numbers or your full tracking history. Share only the information needed to explain the issue.

Need help with your records?

Missing entries or unexpected totals

Describe the tracker, affected date and what you see. Contact support before deleting or reinstalling Upkeep: records are stored locally, and reinstalling may remove data. We cannot promise recovery of lost records.

Removing an accidental entry

Open History in Upkeep and remove the individual entry. Hiding a tracker does not delete its records. See the Privacy Policy’s retention and deletion information for details about local records, recovery copies and backups.

Accounts and syncing

Upkeep does not require an account or provide its own cloud synchronization. Device backups are managed separately by your operating system and backup settings.
